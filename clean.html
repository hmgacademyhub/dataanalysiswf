Why this matters

Outliers use the IQR fence: any value below Q1 - 1.5×IQR or above Q3 + 1.5×IQR is flagged. Winsorising keeps the row but caps the extreme value — usually safer than deletion.

Forward-fill is ideal for time series where missing readings should inherit the previous observation (sensors, stock prices, attendance).
