Master the Data Analysis Workflow

A progressive, cumulative curriculum designed to take you from a raw dataset to professional executive insights.

1

Foundation: The Starting Point

Beginner

Before analyzing data, you must understand what data is and how it's structured. This level focuses on Data Ingestion and the concept of a Tidy Dataset.

  • Understanding CSV vs Excel
  • The Role of Headers and Primary Keys
  • Synchronizing External Sources (Google Sheets)

Practice Task

Upload a sample CSV file and identify how many rows and columns it contains using the Ingest hub.

Go to Ingest Hub
2

Processing: The Art of Cleaning

Intermediate

Raw data is almost always 'dirty'. This level teaches you how to diagnose and fix data quality issues to ensure your analysis is reliable.

  • Detecting Missing Values (Nulls)
  • The 1.5*IQR Rule for Outliers
  • Standardizing Text and Formatting

Practice Task

Load the 'E-Commerce' sandbox and find the column with the highest null percentage. Fill those missing values with a constant.

Go to Cleaning Hub
3

Transformation: Data Shaping

Intermediate+

Once cleaned, data needs to be shaped. We move from basic cleaning to ETL, creating new dimensions and aggregating information.

  • Writing Calculated Columns (Expressions)
  • Unpivoting: Wide to Long Formats
  • Creating Business Logic Rules

Practice Task

Create a new column called 'Total Revenue' by multiplying [Price] and [Quantity] in the ETL hub.

Go to ETL Hub
4

Intelligence: BI & Modelling

Professional

The final step is turning data into stories. We use BI Dashboards and Advanced Models to find patterns and make decisions.

  • KPI Scorecards (Total, Avg, Peak)
  • RFM Analysis for Customer Segmentation
  • Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)

Practice Task

Run a Pareto analysis on your sales data to find the top 20% of products that drive 80% of your revenue.

Go to BI Hub
5

Governance: Ethics & Privacy

Enterprise

Professional analysis requires Accountability. This level covers the legal and ethical frameworks of handling sensitive information.

  • Creating an Audit Trail
  • Data Minimization and Privacy
  • Access Control Matrices

Practice Task

Visit the Governance Hub and review the 'Zero-API Notice' to understand how your data is protected.

Go to Governance Hub
