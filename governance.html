Data Privacy & Zero-API Notice

This platform is engineered with a Zero-API Architecture. Unlike many modern tools, Data Analysis Workflow does not send your uploaded data to any external server or AI API for processing.

  • All data processing occurs in your browser's local RAM.
  • Files are not stored on our servers; they are cleared on page refresh.
  • Your privacy is guaranteed by the architecture itself.
