Why client-side?

Traditional analytics tools (Power BI Service, Tableau Online) require uploading your data to a cloud server. That:

  • Costs you money per seat / per row
  • Adds compliance risk for sensitive data
  • Requires accounts, permissions, and network access

This hub runs entirely in your browser. PapaParse parses CSV, SheetJS reads XLSX, AlaSQL runs SQL, Chart.js draws charts, and IndexedDB persists state — all without a server.
