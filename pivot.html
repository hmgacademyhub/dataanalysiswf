Pivot thinking

A pivot answers “for every X and every Y, what is the aggregated value of Z?”. Choose a categorical column for X (rows) and another for Y (columns), then a numeric column for Z (the measure).

Heatmap shading uses a linear scale from minimum to maximum per cell value. Bright cells = high values. Excellent for finding the “hot regions” in a 2-D distribution.
