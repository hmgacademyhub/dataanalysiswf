Method notes

Linear forecast fits y = slope·x + intercept by least squares on the row index. It is intentionally simple — fast, transparent and explainable. For seasonality, transform your data first (e.g. compute monthly averages in ETL, then forecast).

Moving average smooths random noise so the underlying trend is visible. A window of 7 is good for daily data with weekly cycles.

What-if simply multiplies every numeric cell by (1 + pct/100) into a new column, keeping your original intact. Goal-seek reports the multiplier needed and the implied % change.
